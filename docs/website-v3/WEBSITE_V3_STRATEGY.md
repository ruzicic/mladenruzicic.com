# Website v3 strategy

## Positioning

Primary identity:

```txt
Founder/operator who also leads engineering
```

Secondary identities:

```txt
Staff/principal frontend-platform engineer
Technical product leader
AI-native product engineer / builder
```

Primary line:

```txt
I build products, platforms and the systems around them.
```

Expanded positioning:

```txt
I am a product-minded engineer, technical product leader and founder/operator with deep experience across frontend architecture, mobile apps, developer experience, design systems, product ownership and AI-native execution.

I have worked inside high-scale companies like Shopify and ZF, helped build products from zero, led platform work across multiple teams, and shipped a wide range of independent products across public procurement, education, typography, document archiving, AI tooling and consumer experiments.
```

## Audience hierarchy

1. Senior technical leaders hiring for senior IC, MTS, staff or principal roles
2. Founders and startup operators looking for a high-agency builder
3. Product and engineering leaders looking for technical product leadership
4. Investors and founder-in-residence networks
5. Serious collaborators
6. Selective mentees

## What the site should make obvious

- Mladen can operate as a senior IC or member of technical staff.
- Mladen can operate as a staff or principal engineer.
- Mladen can lead technical product work.
- Mladen can build, validate and operate products from zero.
- Mladen has worked inside high-scale products and inside messy early-stage systems.
- Mladen uses AI deeply and practically, not as a slogan.
- Mladen has learned from successful, paused and stopped projects.

## What the site should repel

Do not optimize for:

- corporate environments
- full-time in-office roles
- agency work
- crypto projects
- people-management-heavy roles
- high-volume mentoring
- generic frontend ticket work

## Messaging hierarchy

### Level 1: Above the fold

- Product engineering
- Technical product leadership
- Founder/operator execution
- AI-native workflows

### Level 2: Evidence

- ZF SCALAR: product ownership, design system, UI platform, adoption
- Shopify: Shop Minis from zero, Shop.app scale, SDK and partner ecosystem
- TenderLift: active AI-native B2B SaaS in Swiss public procurement
- studenti.rs: long-running education platform, bought back and monetized
- FontAlternatives / FontSwap: organic SEO and automation flywheel
- Avataurus, Boxium, MontePop and stopped projects as learning evidence

### Level 3: Depth

- case studies
- timelines
- outcomes
- learnings
- notes and essays
- machine-readable mirrors

## Site goals

- Reposition Mladen from generic developer/mentor to product-minded technical leader and builder.
- Give strong visitors enough evidence to start a serious conversation.
- Make the body of work navigable without flattening everything into one project list.
- Turn independent products into proof of judgment, not random side-project clutter.
- Show AI-native execution through concrete workflows and systems.
- Keep mentoring visible but secondary.
- Make the site readable by humans, search engines and LLMs.

## Non-goals

- No family positioning.
- No family photos.
- No mentorship-first funnel.
- No agency-style CTA.
- No generic portfolio template.
- No cyberpunk AI visual language.
- No CMS unless a real content bottleneck appears.
- No important content hidden behind client-only rendering.

## What to remove from the current site

- Homepage family hero and family alt text.
- Generic `Hello, I'm Mladen` hero.
- Primary description as `software developer, mentor, and entrepreneur`.
- Homepage mentoring pricing.
- Agency-like CTA: `Let's create your next big project together`.
- Hard-coded project list as the source of truth.
- Work page grouping by year as the main structure.

## How the site should make Mladen memorable

Use this angle:

```txt
A builder who moved from frontend execution into platform leadership, then technical product ownership, while independently shipping products across several markets and using AI as a serious operating layer.
```

The memorable contrast:

- high-scale company work plus indie product ownership
- deep frontend/platform expertise plus product judgment
