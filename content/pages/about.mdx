## How I work

I have been shipping software since 2008, starting with PHP sites as a student and
ending up owning product for a platform used across a global company. The through
line is systems: design systems, developer platforms, the tooling and process that
make a team faster than the sum of its people.

I moved between engineering and product on purpose. Owning a roadmap is easier when
you can still read the diff.

## Principles

- Ship something small, then make it right.
- A platform is only real when a second team adopts it.
- Say the number, or say you do not have it.
- Write it down. Most disagreements are vocabulary problems.

## Tools

Next.js and React on the front, TypeScript everywhere, Postgres behind it,
Cloudflare and Vercel in front. AI-assisted from the first line.

## Facts

Based in Lausanne, Switzerland. Originally from Bosnia and Herzegovina. Eighteen
years of shipping. Thirty-plus mentees on MentorCruise.
