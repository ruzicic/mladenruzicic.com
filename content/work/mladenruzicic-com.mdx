## Summary

This website. Every page is generated from one typed content model, which also produces the JSON-LD, the sitemap, the markdown mirrors and the machine-readable index — so the claims cannot drift between what a person reads and what a crawler parses.

## Outcome

Live and open source, and a useful place to try things — content modelling, view transitions, WebGL that degrades properly — where the only person inconvenienced by a bad decision is me.

## Learnings

A personal site is an unusually honest test of taste. There is no brief to hide behind and no stakeholder to blame, so whatever ships is what you actually think good work looks like.
