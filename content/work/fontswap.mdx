## Summary

FontSwap extends FontAlternatives into the browser. A specimen page tells you what a typeface looks like; it does not tell you whether it works in your layout, at your sizes, with your line lengths. The extension swaps the fonts on a live page so the substitution can be judged where it matters.

## Outcome

Live on the Chrome Web Store with a small, real user base. Small numbers, but real ones — an install is a stronger signal than a page view.

## Learnings

Distribution shapes the product. Building for a store means writing for a review process and a permissions prompt, and both of those are part of the design, not paperwork after it.
