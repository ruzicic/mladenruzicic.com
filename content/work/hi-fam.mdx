## Summary

Hi Fam was an attempt at a smaller, closer social platform for music fans, built with a cofounder who had real industry standing and access. That access worked: we ran validation, signed letters of intent and produced content before writing much software.

## Outcome

Stopped after founder alignment broke down. The market signal was genuinely encouraging, which is what makes it worth listing.

## Learnings

Cofounder alignment is infrastructure. It needs to be tested deliberately and early — on how decisions get made and what each person is actually committing — because no amount of early demand compensates for its absence.
