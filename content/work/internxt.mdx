## Summary

A freelance engagement to build the first version of the Internxt Drive mobile app: browsing, uploading and managing files from a phone, against a storage product with an unusual architecture underneath it.

## Outcome

Delivered an initial app that shipped and became the starting point for the product's mobile presence, which the team carried forward after the engagement ended.

## Learnings

Being the first mobile engineer on a product built for the web means the phone exposes every assumption the API made about bandwidth, latency and payload size. Most of the work happens at that boundary, not in the interface.
