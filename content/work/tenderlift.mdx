## Summary

TenderLift watches Swiss public-procurement notices and tells a supplier which
ones are worth their time.

## Role and scope

Solo founder. Product, engineering and operations.

## Context

Swiss public procurement publishes on SIMAP and a spread of cantonal portals.
Small suppliers cannot read all of it, so they miss work they would have won.

## Decisions

- Rank relevance against a structured company profile rather than keywords.
- Summarize requirements in the reader's own language.
- Operate the whole stack with an AI-assisted workflow from the start.
