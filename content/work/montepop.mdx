## Summary

MontePop is an early-stage educational games product with two constraints set deliberately at the start: it works fully offline, and it treats multiple languages as a first-class part of the content model rather than a later localisation pass. Both are much cheaper to design in than to retrofit.

## Outcome

In progress toward an MVP. Nothing to claim yet.

## Learnings

Offline-first is an architectural decision disguised as a feature. Deciding it on day one determines the data model, the sync strategy and the failure modes; deciding it in month six means rewriting all three.
