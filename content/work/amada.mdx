## Summary

A friend rents out a place and needed something better than a listing page: a site of their own, at their own address, that makes the place look the way it looks in person. I designed and built it.

## Role and scope

Solo: content structure, layout, typography, image pipeline, build and deployment. A weekend-sized project in scope, treated with the same care as a larger one, because the standard is the point.

## Context

Guests almost always arrive on a phone, often on a link from a message, often on a poor connection, and they decide in a few seconds. The competition is a well-optimised platform listing. The only advantages an independent site has are speed, better photographs and the absence of clutter.

## Problem

Photography-led sites are usually slow, because photographs are heavy and the temptation is to show all of them at full size. A slow gallery loses the visitor before the first image resolves, which inverts the entire reason for building it.

## Decisions

Static output, no client-side framework, no analytics theatre. Astro renders the pages to HTML and ships effectively no JavaScript, so the first paint is the content.

Images are the product, so they get the engineering: responsive sources, modern formats, explicit dimensions to prevent layout shift, and a deliberate ordering so the strongest photograph is also the first one to load.

Type and spacing carry the tone rather than decoration. For a place people are choosing to sleep in, calm reads as trustworthy and busy reads as a listing.

## Contribution

Everything, from the first layout sketch to the deployed site and the small maintenance that follows.

## Outcome

The site is live and in use. It loads immediately on a phone, works with a mediocre connection, and needs almost nothing from me between seasons.

## Learnings

Small projects are the cleanest place to keep a standard honest. There is no team, no ticket and no reviewer to hide behind, so any shortcut is visibly your own.

Doing a favour well is also a good way to keep a skill sharp. This one took a few days and it is still the fastest thing I have shipped.
