## Summary

Nunium was founded with two friends in Germany. We built an MVP, demoed it to real prospective users and got the kind of response that makes you want to keep going: people understood it immediately and wanted it.

## Outcome

Stopped. The demos showed early demand, but the addressable market was too narrow to justify the continued investment from three people with other commitments.

## Learnings

Positive demos are not a market. Enthusiasm from the first ten people tells you the problem is real; it says nothing about how many more of them exist. Sizing that honestly, early, is cheaper than discovering it after two years — and stopping on a clear-eyed read is a decision, not a defeat.
