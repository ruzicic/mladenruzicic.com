## Summary

Avataurus generates avatars. It started as internal tooling for studenti.rs, was built almost entirely from a phone in short sessions, and required no account and no payment to use — so people used it. It now carries steady, unpromoted traffic.

## Outcome

Live, free, and still running with modest but consistent monthly usage.

## Learnings

The cost of shipping a small idea has collapsed, which changes what is worth trying. This one took hours, not weeks, and it solved a real internal problem before it ever had an outside visitor. That is a good order to do things in.
