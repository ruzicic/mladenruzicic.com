## Summary

Dental practices lose revenue when a chair sits empty because an assistant is out, and qualified assistants want flexible work. FlexMatch was a marketplace between the two, cofounded with someone who had worked inside the industry and could open doors I could not.

## Outcome

An MVP prototype was built and there was real early interest, including signed letters of intent. Stopped after founder alignment broke down.

## Learnings

Domain expertise in a cofounder is a real asset and it accelerated everything — but it does not substitute for agreeing, in writing and early, on direction, commitment and how decisions get made when the two of you disagree.
