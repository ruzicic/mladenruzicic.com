## Summary

An extension that added tags, labels, priorities, counters and a calendar view to Trello boards. Written to scratch my own itch and published to both major extension stores.

## Outcome

Archived and unmaintained for years, while quietly keeping several thousand active users and a top rating. Listed here for the historical record rather than as current work.

## Learnings

Small tools that solve one annoyance precisely can outlive far more ambitious projects, mostly because they never needed anything from their author after launch.
