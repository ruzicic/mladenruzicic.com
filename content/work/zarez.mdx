## Summary

Zarez checks Serbian spelling as you write, in Latin and Cyrillic. It underlines a mistake, explains it in one sentence, and changes nothing until you press Accept.

The everyday check is rules and a dictionary, not a language model, and it runs on your device. A check has to pass a measurement before it may draw a solid line; otherwise it suggests, or stays silent.

The website and the web app went live in November 2026. Sign-in is open to everyone. A Chrome extension and an iPhone keyboard are built and coming soon.
