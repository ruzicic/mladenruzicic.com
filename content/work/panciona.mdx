## Where it started

It started in 2019 as a freelance job for one nutritionist: a tool to keep her clients, their plans and their check-ins in one place. She then helped me turn it into a product for other people like her.

## What it was

Panciona was made for coaches, trainers and nutritionists. Each of them got an app for their own clients, fully white-labelled, on their own domain, where they could share programmes and sell them. The website's headline was "Make a living selling fitness programs online".

## The programme board

A programme is a board: one column per week or per day, each a stack of cards for notes, exercises and recipes that you drag into place. The same board makes a four-week strength block or a seven-day meal plan, and a coach can give it to a client for a set period.

## Selling a programme

Any programme could have a price and a public page. Someone opens the page, signs up with their email and a six-digit code, and the coach approves them from the dashboard.

## What happened

Product-market fit never came. Four customers paid. I underestimated how hard it is to reach fitness influencers, and I was the wrong founder for that market.

## Alongside

I ran it for about a year and a half while working at Shopify, which was nothing but supportive. In that time I also finished [Innosuisse's Business Creation for Social Entrepreneurs course at Startup Campus](https://www.innosuisse.admin.ch/en/entrepreneurship-training).

## The website

The website still builds from its repository, so these are its real pages, not a mock-up.

## Stack

React and Node.js on Postgres for the app, Next.js and Tailwind for the website.
