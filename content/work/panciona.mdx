## Summary

Panciona was a bootstrapped SaaS I designed, built and shipped by myself. It worked. It did not find product-market fit, and looking back the clearest reason is that my fit with that market was weak: I had no particular insight into the buyers and no route to reach them that did not start from zero.

## Outcome

Stopped. No sustainable traction, and the honest read was that continuing meant paying tuition on a lesson I had already learned.

## Learnings

Building the product is the easy part. Owning the market — knowing the buyer, having a distribution path, being credible in the domain — is the part that decides the outcome, and it is not a phase that starts after launch. Every product I have started since begins with the buyer.
