## Summary

Boxium addressed Swiss record-retention obligations: keeping the right documents, for the required period, in a form that survives an audit. The customer discovery was solid and the domain is unglamorous in the way that usually signals a real market.

## Outcome

Paused because of time constraints while TenderLift took the available hours. The research and the positioning are still valid, and it is worth restarting.

## Learnings

Two Swiss B2B compliance products at once is one too many for a person with a full-time job. Choosing the one with the clearer buyer was the right call, and pausing the other deliberately is better than starving both.
